This thesis studies an underwater target detection and sensing system based on the polarization band effect. The work focuses on the spatiotemporal distribution of charge signals generated by underwater targets in complex physical environments, and combines MEMS charge sensor arrays, simulation modeling, experimental validation, and machine-learning-based signal analysis.
